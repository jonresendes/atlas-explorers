Atlas Explorers Privacy Policy

Last updated: 21 November 2026

Who we are

Atlas Explorers is a geography game for children and families, made by Jonathan Resendes. Questions about this policy: [your contact email].

The short version

  • No ads, no in-app purchases, no advertising or cross-app tracking, and no chat between players.
  • We count how many devices use the game, anonymously, so we know which games are played. This can be switched off.
  • You can play without an account. Progress then stays on your device.
  • If you make a player to save progress online, we keep only a made-up player name, a password (handled by Google Firebase sign-in) and your game progress.
  • We never ask for a real name, email address, age, photo or location.

Information kept on your device

Scores, stars, stamps, badges, settings and the chosen passport are saved in your browser's or app's local storage. They stay on the device and can be removed by clearing the app's data.

Information kept online (signed-in players only)

  • Player name and password. Used only to sign in. Passwords are stored by Google Firebase Authentication; the game never sees or stores them. Player names are not email addresses.
  • Game progress. Scores, stars, badges and the chosen passport, so a player can carry on from any device.
  • Online leaderboard. A name tag of up to 6 characters, the score, what was achieved (for example "8/10 correct") and how long it took. These are visible to other players. Players can switch this off in Settings → Privacy & safety, which also removes their existing leaderboard entries.

This information is stored with Google Firebase (Cloud Firestore and Firebase Authentication). Security rules allow each player to read and change only their own progress.

Bug reports

Anyone can report a problem from inside the game. A report is only sent when the player taps Send report, and it contains: what they wrote, a picture of the game screen if they chose to add one, the game version, the page or game they were on, the type of device and browser, and the screen size. Players are asked not to write personal details. Reports can only be read by the game's maker, and are deleted once they're no longer needed.

Anonymous usage counts

To see how many people play and which games they use, each device gets a random anonymous number when the game first opens. With it we keep: the dates the game was opened, how many games were finished, the game version, whether it's the installed app or a web browser, and the type of device (for example "iPhone" or "Windows"). There are no names, no locations and nothing that identifies who is playing, and this information is never shared or used for advertising. It can be switched off at any time in Settings → Privacy & safety → Share anonymous usage counts.

Voice answers

Some games let players answer out loud. This is optional and the microphone is only on while the 🎤 button is lit. Speech is turned into words by the device's or browser's own speech recognition service (for example Google in Chrome, Apple in Safari). Atlas Explorers does not record, store or send audio anywhere itself.

Children's privacy

Atlas Explorers is designed for children. We do not knowingly collect personal information that identifies a child, and usage counts are anonymous. Player names are filtered for inappropriate words, there is no way for players to contact each other, and links that leave the game are behind a grown-up check.

Deleting information

In the game, open Settings → Privacy & safety → Delete [player] forever. This permanently deletes the player's sign-in, online progress and leaderboard entries. You can also ask us to delete a player by contacting jon.resendes@gmail.com with the player name.

Sharing

We do not sell or share information with anyone. Google Firebase stores the online information on our behalf.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date.
